# Training Montage - Design Concept

## Design Philosophy

**Clinical reasoning is a muscle. You're in the gym doing reps.**

Every case is a workout. Track your progress, build streaks, level up. Training, not testing.

## Key Features

- **Dark, Motivational Aesthetic**: Gym-like atmosphere with gritty, energetic visuals
- **Reps Counter**: Every case is a "rep" toward the 1000-case goal
- **Streak System**: 🔥 Fire emoji for daily streaks, gamified progress
- **Levels**: Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert based on reps
- **Micro-Feedback**: Instant "Rep counted" / "Good form" messages after each action
- **Progress Metrics**: "Your pattern recognition is 23% sharper than last week"

## What Problem Does It Solve?

**Primary Issue**: Users don't realize they need to do 1000 cases to build clinical reasoning. They want quick wins.

**Solution**: Frames the entire journey as training montage. You're Rocky. Every rep counts. Small wins are celebrated. Momentum is visible.

## User Testing Questions

1. **Does this make 1000 cases feel achievable?** Do they talk about "reps" or "training"?
2. **Do they want to "go again"?** After feedback, do they click "Go Again" or "Rest"?
3. **Does gamification work?** Do streaks and levels motivate them?
4. **Do competitive students respond?** This concept targets goal-oriented learners.

## Design Details

- **Color Palette**: Dark mode (#1a1a1a, #2d2d2d) with orange/gold accents (#ff6b35, #ffd23f)
- **Typography**: Bold, uppercase, high-impact fonts
- **Layout**: Minimalist case screen, focus on action and feedback
- **Tone**: Motivational, energetic, competitive

## Best For

Competitive, goal-oriented students who respond to gamification and metrics. Those who like Duolingo streaks, fitness apps, and visible progress tracking.
